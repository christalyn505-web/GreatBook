Introduction

A book of stories that came first.

Long before paper and printing presses, these tales were passed from mouth to mouth, from grandparents to grandchildren, beside the cooking fire and under the mango trees. They are the stories that shaped how we see the world, and the ground every great book after them was built on.

Scroll
01, The Collection

Fables and legends from the Philippines.

Every story here is drawn from the culture, traditions, and customs of a different corner of the country. These are not only stories for children, they are a record of who we are as a people.

02, The Illustrations

Each tale is drawn by its own group.

Every story carries original artwork made by the members of the group that wrote it. The illustrator is named beneath each picture, alongside the writer and the narrator.

03, The Narration

You can listen to every story.

Each page carries a recorded narration. Press play and let the story be read to you, the way it was always meant to be heard.

04, The Quiz

And at the end, a short test.

Every story is followed by a brief quiz to check what you took from it. Five questions, answered right here on the page, scored the moment you finish. Let's begin.
